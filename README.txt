PORTFOLIO REVIEW PACKAGE — Christopher Ongko
Solarpunk-bitcoin research portfolio, July 2026

Contents:

1. Constrained_Ledger_JOURNAL.pdf / .docx
   "The Constrained Ledger: When Does Energy Actually Discipline Digital Money?"
   Journal-length working paper (13 pages). Tests whether Bitcoin's cumulative
   mining cost disciplines its price (it does not, by four falsifiable tests),
   then builds and tests a five-condition rule system for energy-linked
   financial claims, including execution on a public blockchain testnet.
   Not yet submitted anywhere.

2. Constrained_Ledger_FULL_THESIS.pdf
   Full-length thesis version (31 pages) of the same underlying work —
   7 chapters, appendices, full reference list. Set aside in favor of the
   journal version for near-term use; included for reference/comparison.

3. Constrained_Ledger_PLAIN_LANGUAGE.pdf
   A plain-language rewrite of the same paper (25 pages), aimed at general
   readers rather than an academic audience. Separate document, not a
   replacement for the journal version.

4. Invisible_Ledger_CORRECTED.pdf / .docx
   "The Invisible Ledger: Quantifying ASEAN's $170 Billion Unmeasured
   Platform Economy." Estimates untaxed platform-participant income across
   five ASEAN countries via audited financial filings, validated with a
   difference-in-differences natural experiment (Malaysia's 2024 tax base
   expansion). Accepted for presentation at the National Tax Association's
   119th Annual Conference (Pittsburgh, Nov 2026). This corrected draft
   documents and fixes a sourcing error from an earlier version in full.

5. ECI_FINAL.pdf / .docx
   "Energy Circulation as an Economic Indicator: What Tracks the Real
   Economy, What Doesn't, and What's New." Tests three candidate
   energy-linked economic indicators (crypto/DeFi activity, general
   electricity generation, sector-specific electricity demand) against
   real data with multiple-testing correction. Not yet submitted anywhere.

Author's own connection: Christopher is the author of all three papers and
is also connected to the SPK/Constrained Ledger project referenced in
paper #1 (disclosed directly in that paper).
